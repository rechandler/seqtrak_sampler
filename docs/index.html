The problem

Your resamples are stuck in .Y3S files

The SEQTRAK can resample itself, but the result can only live on the sampler track. Getting it onto a synth engine meant re-recording it somewhere else.

Before

  1. Resample on the SEQTRAK
  2. Open a DAW and set up the SEQTRAK as an input
  3. Play the sample back and record it again
  4. Trim, normalize and export a WAV by hand
  5. Import the WAV into a synth track

With SEQTRAK Sampler

  1. Resample on the SEQTRAK
  2. Export the sound from the SEQTRAK app's Sound Manager
  3. Drag the .Y3S into SEQTRAK Sampler. Silence is trimmed for you.
  4. Click Export WAV
  5. Import the WAV into a synth track
Features

Built for one job, done well

The audio comes straight out of the SEQTRAK's own file. Nothing is re-recorded, so the WAV is a bit-for-bit copy of what the hardware captured.

Lossless extraction

Reads the 16-bit, 44.1 kHz audio inside the .Y3S directly, with no resampling and no generation loss.

Visual trimming

Silence is cut automatically. Drag the start and end handles on the stereo waveform to fine-tune.

Hear before you export

Press Play or Space to hear exactly the region that will be saved, normalized and faded.

16-second guard

A meter shows the selection against the SEQTRAK's limit. Over-long sounds are flagged before they fail on import.

Normalize & de-click

Optional peak normalization to -1 dB, and short fades wherever a handle cuts through sound.

Batch & command line

Load a whole session and export all at once, or script it with the bundled y3s2wav tool.

# command line
y3s2wav *.Y3S -o wavs/ --normalize
FAQ

Questions

Where do I get the .Y3S file?

In the SEQTRAK app, open Project / Sound Manager, go to Sounds, right-click the resampled sound and choose Export.

Does it change my SEQTRAK or its library?

No. SEQTRAK Sampler only reads .Y3S files and writes ordinary WAV files to a folder you choose. It never writes Yamaha files or touches the SEQTRAK app's data.

Why is there a 16-second limit?

That's the SEQTRAK's own maximum sample length. The app warns you and blocks the export until the selection fits, or you can press "Cut to 16 s" to trim it with a fade-out.

Is the output stereo?

Yes, always 44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo. Mono sounds are copied to both channels.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

The builds aren't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer certificate yet. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open. On Windows, click More info, then Run anyway. You can also build it yourself from source.

How does it read .Y3S files?

A .Y3S export is a zip archive holding XML metadata and raw PCM audio. The layout is documented in FORMAT.md.
